Compound scores

One row per treated compound, with the two summary scores Table S8 of Asp et al., 2026 (iScience 29, 116673, CC BY 4.0) publishes for this screen. Table S8 scores each compound at several doses; each row shows the dose with the highest morphology score, and the infection rate and cell count measured at that same dose. A low infection rate with a low cell count can mean the cells died rather than that they were protected.

Two of the four scores requested are absent, and that is not a missing result: the cytopathic-effect (CPE) assay was not run on this cell line, and phospholipidosis is deferred. Compounds outside Table S8 read "not reported", never zero — the validation screen scored only the compounds the primary screen carried forward.

"Hits only" applies Table S8's own threshold, a morphology score above {{ compound_table.hit_threshold }}; the thresholds to filter at are still an open question to the research group. No threshold is preset for the infection rate: none is published for this cell line, and the 59.7 % cut-off in the paper belongs to the primary Vero E6 screen.
